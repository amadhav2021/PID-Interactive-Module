How do drones control themselves?

This sounds like an easy question, but if you dig a little deeper, it's actually quite a challenge. Imagine you have a drone and you want it to hover at a certain height. You might input a target altitude, but the drone can only control its propellers. How does it translate the target height into a command for the propellers?

The simplest strategy is to just turn the propellers on when the drone is below the target altitude, and turn them off when the drone is above. Seems logical, right? Use the simulator below to see how that would play out:

Analysis

This type of controller is called a bang-bang controller, because it switches between 2 inputs (thrust on and thrust off). While the logic is simple, the results aren't great. Let's think about why the drone behaves the way it does.

As soon as the drone hits the target height, the propellers shut off. But the drone still has some upward momentum, so it overshoots the target a bit before falling back down due to gravity. Once the drone falls below the target height, the propellers turn back on, causing the drone to oscillate around the target. How can we fix this?

What if we ease the propeller speed as the drone gets closer to the target height? When the drone is far away, the propellers can be at full thrust, and as the drone gets closer to the target, the propellers slow down. Let's try it!
